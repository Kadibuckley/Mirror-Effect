The Mirror Effect: Empowering the Next Generation Through Identity-Matched Mentoring

At Reading Memorial High School (RMHS), 87% of our Black students aspire to attend a 4-year university. However, an opportunity gap persists: while 72% of White students are enrolled in advanced courses, only 31% of Black students are currently enrolled. When asked about barriers, 37.5% shared: "I don't think I can do it."

Our identity-matched mentoring program is grounded in Bandura’s Theory of Self-Efficacy. Designed to bridge the gap between current academic choices and future career goals, identity-matched mentors serve as vicarious models who help students realize they belong in Advanced Placement, dual-enrollment courses, and executive professional spaces.
